# promptmaxxer brand

## Name
- Always lowercase: **promptmaxxer**. Never "PromptMaxxer" or "Prompt Maxxer".
- Verb: "maxx" (to maxx a prompt). Result: "maxxed".

## Promise
- Tagline: **Help your output ASCEND** ("ASCEND" always in capitals).
- One line: Turn any rough prompt into a great one, in any app, in about two seconds.
- Do not use "two seconds" in public copy until the build measures it (spec target: p50 under 2.5 s).

## Mark and icons
- Mark: a Greek statue head in profile, black and white only. The classic looksmaxxing icon. File `mark.svg` (vector).
- App icon: `app-icon.svg`, black Apple-grid body, white statue.
- Menu bar icon: `menubar-icon.svg`. Blurs at 18 px and at 16 px: a simpler small-size statue is still to make.
- Wordmark: `wordmark.svg`, statue plus "promptmaxxer" in Inter ExtraBold, black.
- Logo colours: black and white only. Never put the logo in Volt or Ultraviolet.

## Type
- Inter ExtraBold for headlines and the wordmark. Inter Regular for body. Both free (SIL Open Font License).
- JetBrains Mono for prompts, keys and paths. Free (SIL OFL).
- Inside the Mac app, menus and settings use the system font so the app feels native.

## Voice
- Short, confident, a little playful. Verbs first. Plain words.
- App messages: "Maxxed. Pasted in place." · "Highlight some text first." · "Couldn't maxx this one. Your text is unchanged."
- Never: hype ("unlock the power of AI"), emoji in the app, unproven claims ("10x better"), more than one exclamation mark.

## The moment
The floating bar is the product's signature: a dark Panel pill beside the selection, the mark, "Maxxing for <target>", a context chip in Ultraviolet ("shop · CLAUDE.md + 2 memories"), the prompt streaming in JetBrains Mono with a Volt cursor, and a Ultraviolet-to-Volt progress line.
